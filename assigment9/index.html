Features of Igbo (Nigeria) Traditional Marriage

Wine

The bride and groom exchange wine as a symbol of their commitment to each other.

Dance

The couple performs traditional dances to celebrate their union.

Food

Rich traditional dishes are served to celebrate the occasion.

About the Ceremony

At the heart of Igbo traditional marriage is the Igba Nkwu, the symbolic search for the groom. Guided by the blessings of her parents, the bride takes a calabash cup (iko-nkwu) filled with fresh palm wine and moves through the joyful crowd to find her husband. Kneeling before him to offer the wine—and his acceptance by drinking from the cup—publicly seals their covenant of love, mutual respect, and lifelong partnership before both families and the community.
